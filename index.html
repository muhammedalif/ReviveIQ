Pathway-aware (AHA 2025 ACLS): After each rhythm check, select Shockable or Non-shockable. The app then adjusts drug prompts and antiarrhythmic reminders accordingly.

Shockable (VF/pVT): Adrenaline every 4 min from shock #1. Amiodarone 300mg (or Lidocaine 1–1.5mg/kg) after shock #3. Amiodarone 150mg (or Lidocaine 0.5–0.75mg/kg) after shock #5.

Non-shockable (PEA/Asystole): Adrenaline ASAP, then every 4 min (alternate rhythm checks). No antiarrhythmic.

CCF: Code Blue time = denominator. Compressions tracked only when "Start compressions" is pressed. AHA target ≥80%. 15s advance warnings for all intervals.

Saving the report: Tap "Download report" to save a .html file. On Android, open the file and use Share → Print to save as PDF. On iPhone, open in Safari and tap Share → Print → Pinch outward on preview to save as PDF.

⚠ For trained medical professionals only. Always follow institutional protocols and team leader directives.

Reset session?

All timers, counters, pathway data, CCF, and the event log will be cleared. This cannot be undone.
